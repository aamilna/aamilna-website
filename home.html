The Heart of AaMilNa

Meet Cherry Blossom

More than just a mascot, Cherry was a real cat with a gentle soul who taught us the true meaning of kindness, sharing, and unconditional love.

Found wandering as a hungry kitten, she grew up alongside our founder. Though she couldn't speak, her actions spoke volumes—sharing her food with other animals, comforting those who were sad, and always trusting completely.

Though she is no longer with us, her spirit lives on in every corner of AaMilNa. Just as she guided our founder, our mascot Cherry is here to guide you through our marketplace, reminding us that every creation is made with love, and every interaction should be filled with kindness.
